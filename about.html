about

hey! i'm kenar — a first-year at oxford reading comp sci & philosophy at christ church.

i grew up between loughborough, oxford, and jakarta, which gave me an odd mix of influences. i ended up at the intersection of two things i can't stop thinking about: formal systems and the big philosophical questions — consciousness, computation, what understanding actually is.

fractals are something i keep coming back to. there's something almost philosophically interesting about infinite structure arising from a few lines of iteration — it feels like a clue about how complexity works in general.

i also think the questions around AI and interpretability are the most urgent right now. not just engineering questions — what is it that these systems are doing? does the hard problem of consciousness have anything to say here?

when i'm not in tutorials, i'm probably wandering christ church meadow or going down a rabbit hole on some fractal geometry paper.
